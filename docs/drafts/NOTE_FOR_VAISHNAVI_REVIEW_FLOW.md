# For Vaishnavi: the review after teaching, a proposed change (14 Nov 2026)

Ramesh and I would like your view before we change it in the app. Please answer the questions in **bold** at the end, briefly; a word per question is enough.

## Why change it

The Review now goes in a fixed order: (1) **पहला अनुमान** for every group, (2) **बच्चों का काम** (observing, reading aloud, worksheet photos), (3) **अंतिम निर्णय** for every group. In a real day this order breaks:
- Observing and reading aloud now happen **during You Do**, from a button inside the group's "तुम करोगे". So the children's work is often recorded **before** the first judgment.
- The teacher may not have time in class: she may mark A / PA / NA on the paper tick sheet, or take the worksheet photos later, and enter everything after class.
- The hackathon evaluators also found the step labels unclear.

## The proposal: two phases

**During teaching** (on आज की योजना, inside each group's slot; everything optional):
- Observe and mark A / PA / NA now, or on the paper tick sheet.
- Reading aloud: listen to the **3-4 children named for today** (rotating, so a group of 12 is fully heard in 3-4 days). The screen says: "आज 4 बच्चे · बाकी अगले दिनों में". *(Ramesh has already agreed to this.)*
- Worksheet photos now, if there is time (her judgment first, see below).
- Tick **"इस समूह को पढ़ा दिया"** when the group is done.

**After teaching** (on समीक्षा, any time **the same day**, groups in any order). One card per group:
1. **आपका अनुमान: यह समूह कैसा रहा?** — आगे बढ़ें / दोबारा पढ़ाएँ / पक्का नहीं (one tap), from her overall sense of the lesson.
2. **बच्चों का काम** — what is recorded and what is left (enter from the paper sheet, take the photos).
3. **कल के लिए निर्णय** — आगे बढ़ें / दोबारा पढ़ाएँ / पक्का नहीं, with her judgment and the evidence side by side. Tomorrow's plan follows this.

**When the judgment is asked:**
- For a **photo** group it is **required before the photos are sent** (in class or after), so the AI's reading never shapes her own view first.
- For **observed and read-aloud** groups it is asked before she enters the children's marks; one tap, and she may skip it. If she has already marked the children in class, it is still asked, marked "रिकॉर्ड के बाद" (after recording).
- The mentor report's line "पहला अनुमान जाँच से मेल खाया" counts **only judgments made before that group's evidence**, for all three kinds of group.
