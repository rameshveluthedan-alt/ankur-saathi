Anchor (whole class)

गाँव का बाज़ार

आज हम गाँव के बाज़ार चलेंगे। रानी को अपनी माँ के लिए फल खरीदने हैं। बोर्ड पर देखें: आम 24 रुपये, केला 35 रुपये, अमरूद 18 रुपये।

Opening: बाज़ार में कौन-कौन से फल हैं? कौन सा फल सबसे महँगा लग रहा है?

  • शुरुआती · Beginners: बोर्ड पर बाज़ार की कितनी चीज़ें हैं, गिनें। फिर आम और केले के दाम में दहाई और इकाई के अंक अलग-अलग पहचानें।
  • प्रगतिशील · Progressors: रानी ने आम और केला खरीदा। कुल कितने पैसे लगे? कॉपी में 24 + 35 जोड़ें। फिर केला और अमरूद का कुल दाम जोड़ें।
  • दक्ष · Proficients: रानी के पास 100 रुपये हैं। उसने आम, केला और अमरूद खरीदा। कुल कितने रुपये लगे और कितने बचे? कॉपी में लिखकर हल करें।

Closing: सबसे महँगा फल कौन सा था? सबसे सस्ता कौन सा? सब बच्चों ने बहुत अच्छा सोचा!

Why this plan?

Why these three groups
Beginners build the prerequisite, Progressors work on today's carry skill with support, and Proficients already have the carry so they extend to 3-digit addition.
Why this rotation order
Beginners go first and longest for foundation work with sticks. Progressors follow for today's key skill. Proficients get a short block since they already know carrying.
Mistakes to watch for
Progressor problems 2 and 3 catch the forgotten carry and the full ones-sum written in one column. Beginner problems 2 and 3 catch column misalignment. Proficient problems 2 and 3 catch the dropped tens-to-hundreds carry.
Use of sticks
With no printer, worksheet problems are copied from the board into notebooks. Beginners use sticks in pairs, Progressors see the carry on the teacher's kit, and Proficients use the written method.
Teaching and Learning Resources
**Anchor activity (whole class):** 'Village Market Story' was written for this very skill; it was adapted to today's numbers.
**For each group:**
• **Beginners** ('Adds two 2-digit numbers without regrouping'): hands-on learning with the stick pair kits.
• **Progressors** ('Adds two 2-digit numbers with regrouping (carry-over)'): the steps of 'Carry-over Re-teaching with Bundling' form the basis for I Do and We Do, followed by You Do (worksheet practice) to build conceptual clarity and prevent errors.
• **Proficients** ('Adds 3-digit numbers with regrouping'): practice with the written method on the board, no sticks.
**Materials (to prepare):** Bundling sticks: a teacher demo kit of 99 sticks and 4 pair kits for the Beginners.
**State plan note:** THE most misconception-rich competency. Bundling stick demonstration essential.
**Weekly plan:** the app uses a representative weekly plan for now, not the state's official one.

Why this plan?

Why these three groups
Beginners work on addition without carrying, Progressors on today's goal (with carrying), and Proficients on 3-digit addition, so each child learns at their own level.
Why this rotation order
Beginners go first with the longest slot because they need the most support on the foundation skill; then Progressors on today's goal, then Proficients.
Mistakes to watch for
Watch Progressors for forgetting the carry and writing the full ones sum; Beginners for misaligned columns; Proficients for dropping the tens-to-hundreds carry.
Assumptions in today's plan
With no printer, worksheet problems are copied from the board. No gaps were recorded yesterday, so there is no re-teach block; the Progressor lesson uses the bundle-stick method to prevent carry mistakes in advance.
Teaching and Learning Resources

Why this plan?

Why these three groups
Beginners work on the prerequisite (no carry), Progressors on today's target (carry), and Proficients are secure in carrying so they extend to 3 digits.
Why this rotation order
Beginners go first with the longest block since they need the most support and hands-on sticks. Progressors follow while the teacher is fresh for the hardest topic. Proficients go last as the most independent group.
Mistakes to watch for
Beginners: adding ones and tens in the wrong column. Progressors: forgetting the carry (answer 10 less) or writing the full 2-digit ones sum. Proficients: dropping the tens-to-hundreds carry. Worksheet problems 2 and 3 are designed to reveal these.
Assumption for today's plan
There is no printer, so worksheet problems are copied from the board into notebooks. Stick bundles must be made before class since children never bundle during the lesson.
Teaching and Learning Resources
**Anchor activity (whole class):** 'Village Market Story' was written for this very skill; it was adapted to today's numbers.
**For each group:**
• **Beginners** ('Adds two 2-digit numbers without regrouping'): hands-on learning with the stick pair kits.
• **Progressors** ('Adds two 2-digit numbers with regrouping (carry-over)'): the steps of 'Carry-over Re-teaching with Bundling' form the basis for I Do and We Do, followed by You Do (worksheet practice) to build conceptual clarity and prevent errors.
• **Proficients** ('Adds 3-digit numbers with regrouping'): practice with the written method on the board, no sticks.
**Materials (to prepare):** Bundling sticks: a teacher demo kit of 99 sticks and 4 pair kits for the Beginners.

Why this plan?

Why these three groups
Today's goal L2.7.2 belongs to the Progressors. Beginners first learn L1.7.3 (spacing between words), and Proficients extend to L3.7.1 (a paragraph).
Why this rotation order
Beginners go first with the longest slot because they need the foundation. Progressors get 14 minutes for today's main skill, and Proficients get a short 7-minute framing and practice block.
Mistakes to watch
For Progressors, watch for sentences left unjoined, missing full stops and repeated openings. For Proficients, watch for drifting off topic and lack of order.
Assumption for today's plan
Without a printer, worksheets are copied from the board into notebooks. It is assumed children can copy from the board. Progressor and Proficient writing will be finished at their own place in the next block.
Teaching and Learning Resources

Why this plan?

Why these three groups
Beginners build the spacing foundation, Progressors work on today's goal with support, and Proficients move on to paragraph writing.
Why this rotation order
Beginners get the first and longest slot because they need the most support and everything else builds on this base.
Mistakes to watch
Watch Progressors for disconnected sentences, missing full stops and repeated openings; watch Proficients for going off-topic, no sequence, and too few sentences.
Assumptions specific to today's plan
With no printer, worksheets are copied from the board into notebooks. Since this is a writing skill, each group is checked from their written notebook work, photographed after class.

Why this plan?

Why these three groups
Beginners are still learning to leave spaces between words. Progressors work on today's goal with support, and Proficients extend to a 5-7 sentence paragraph.
Why this rotation order
The Beginners get the first and longest slot (29 minutes) because they need the most teacher support for the foundation skill. Progressors follow, and the most independent Proficients go last.
Mistakes to watch
With Progressors, watch for standalone sentences, missing full stops, and every sentence starting the same way. With Proficients, watch for drifting off topic and a missing order and ending.
Assumptions for today
There is no printer, so worksheets are written on the board and children solve them in notebooks. The written questions are kept short so that copying them onto the board takes little time.
Teaching and Learning Resources
**Anchor activity (whole class):** 'Letter from a Friend' was written for this very skill; it was adapted to today's skill.
**For each group:**
• **Beginners** ('Writes simple sentences (3-5 words) with spaces between words'): practice on a worksheet at their own level.
• **Progressors** ('Writes 3-5 connected sentences on a familiar topic'): I Do and We Do, followed by You Do (worksheet practice) to build conceptual clarity and prevent errors.
• **Proficients** ('Writes a coherent paragraph of 5-7 sentences on a given topic'): written practice on the board and the worksheet.

Why this plan?

Why these three groups
All three groups work on the same competency at different levels. Beginners revise sorting by one rule (colour), Progressors choose the rule themselves, and Proficients re-sort the same objects by two different rules.
Why this rotation order
Beginners go first and get the longest slot because they need the most support. Proficients get the shortest block because they can work more independently.
What to watch for
No common mistakes are recorded for this skill, so watch whether the child sticks to one rule throughout or switches midway, and whether the child can state the rule aloud.
Assumptions for today's plan
The plan assumes everyday objects like pebbles, buttons and caps are available. There is no printer, so worksheets are a back-up copied from the board. A/PA/NA is decided by the teacher's observation.
Anchor match
No anchor template matched today's competency (sorting). The shape-hunt template was adapted loosely for its classroom setting and format, and every group's task was rewritten to practise sorting.

कक्षा में चीज़ें छाँटो

आज हम अपनी कक्षा की चीज़ों को छाँटेंगे। देखें, मेरे पास कुछ चीज़ें हैं: कुछ लाल हैं, कुछ नीली। हम इन्हें अलग-अलग ढेर में रखेंगे।

Opening: इन चीज़ों को हम किस आधार पर अलग-अलग रख सकते हैं? रंग से, आकार से, या बड़े-छोटे से?

  • शुरुआती · Beginners: शुरुआती: शिक्षक एक चीज़ उठाएँ। बच्चे उसका रंग बताएँ और बताएँ कि वह किस ढेर में जाएगी। फिर बच्चे अपनी जगह से हाथ उठाकर 'लाल ढेर' या 'नीला ढेर' दिखाएँ।
  • प्रगतिशील · Progressors: प्रगतिशील: बच्चे अपनी जगह से बताएँ कि छाँटने का नियम क्या है (रंग, आकार या बड़ा-छोटा)। एक बच्चा बताए कि कौन सी चीज़ किस ढेर में जाएगी।
  • दक्ष · Proficients: दक्ष: बच्चे बताएँ कि एक ही चीज़ों को दो अलग नियमों से भी छाँटा जा सकता है, जैसे पहले रंग से, फिर आकार से। 'अब नियम बदलकर छाँटें' कहकर उन्हें सोचने को कहें।

Closing: आज हमने किस आधार पर चीज़ें छाँटीं? हर जोड़ी अपना नियम ज़ोर से बताएगी।

Why this plan?

Why these three groups
All three groups work on the same skill (sorting), so Beginners sort by one clear colour rule, Progressors identify the rule themselves, and Proficients try more than one rule.
Why this rotation order
Beginners get the first and longest slot because they need the most guidance on the foundation; the other two groups practise in pairs until their turn.
What to watch for
No specific mistakes are recorded for this skill. Watch whether the child sorts randomly or by a rule, and whether they can say the rule aloud. This skill is observed, so the worksheet is practice only.
Assumptions specific to today
No anchor template matched today's competency; the one used was adapted loosely, keeping only its classroom setting. No mastered skill is recorded for any group, so independent work is a simple sorting review. With no printer, the worksheets are copied from the board.

All three groups share one skill (sorting) at different levels: Beginners learn one rule, Progressors sort 10 objects with support, Proficients sort by two rules.

Why this plan?

Why these three groups
Beginners work on the prerequisite simple sentence reading (L1.5.1). Progressors work on today's competency (L2.5.1). Proficients extend to L3.5.1.
Why this rotation order
Beginners go first with the longest block as they need the foundation. Progressors, working on today's target, get the second-longest block. Proficients read independently well, so they get the shortest block.
Mistakes to watch
In the Progressor group, word-by-word reading (L2.5.1-M1) is the most common. Also watch for fast reading with skipped words (L2.5.1-M3). While listening, focus on phrasing.
Assumption for today's plan
Today's check is the teacher's one-minute oral reading, so worksheets are practice only. There is no time to hear every child, so only the children shown in the app are heard.

जीभ का कसरत

आज हम अपनी जीभ की कसरत करेंगे। बोर्ड पर एक मज़ेदार पंक्ति लिखी है: 'छह छोटे छाते छत पर छाए'। पहले धीरे-धीरे पढ़ें या पढ़ें, फिर आप सब दोहराएँगे, फिर थोड़ा तेज़।

Opening: इस पंक्ति में कौन सी आवाज़ बार-बार आ रही है?

  • शुरुआती · Beginners: पंक्ति सुनकर बोलकर दोहराएँ। फिर बताएँ: 'कौन सी आवाज़ बार-बार आ रही है?' (उत्तर: 'छ')।
  • प्रगतिशील · Progressors: बोर्ड से पंक्ति पढ़ें। जो अक्षर बार-बार आता है, उसे कॉपी में गोला लगाएँ। फिर पंक्ति तीन बार तेज़ बोलने की कोशिश करें।
  • दक्ष · Proficients: 'छ' या किसी और आवाज़ से अपनी एक छोटी जीभ-कसरत पंक्ति बनाकर कॉपी में लिखें और कक्षा को पढ़कर सुनाएँ।

Closing: कौन सी पंक्ति सबसे मुश्किल थी? हाथ उठाकर बताएँ और वोट दें।

Why this plan?

Why these three groups
Beginners are still building simple-sentence reading (L1.5.1), Progressors are at today's passage-reading goal (L2.5.1), and Proficients are extending toward faster, more accurate reading (L3.5.1).
Why this rotation order
Beginners get the first and longest slot so they receive the most direct support. The Progressor lesson falls in the middle while children are still fresh and can concentrate.
Which mistakes to watch
The most common Progressor mistake is word-by-word reading (L2.5.1-M1), so stress phrasing. Also watch for children who read fast but skip or swap words (L2.5.1-M3).
Assumptions specific to today's plan
Without a printer, worksheets are copied from the board. For the fluency check, hear one minute from each child the app shows; hearing every child in one day is not feasible.

Why this plan?

Why these three groups
Beginners are still learning simple sentence reading, Progressors are working on today's passage-reading goal, and Proficients are going beyond to faster, more accurate reading. Each group learns at its own level.
Why this rotation order
Beginners go first and get the longest slot because they need the most teacher support and learn best while fresh. Progressors follow, then Proficients.
Mistakes to watch for
In the Progressor group watch for two things: word-by-word reading without phrasing, and reading too fast while skipping or misreading words. No recorded mistakes exist for the Beginner and Proficient skills, so listen to their reading directly.
Assumption for today's plan
Reading is checked by the teacher hearing each selected child read for one minute, so the worksheet is practice, not the check. No re-teach was recorded from yesterday, so no separate re-teach block is included.
