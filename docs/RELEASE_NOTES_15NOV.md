# What's new on main (live since 15 Nov 2026, revision ankur-saathi-00017)

Ankur Saathi has been updated from the Phase 2 evaluators' feedback, ahead of the 18 Nov finals demo. There are 17 changes, all tested in the lab first.

## Simpler screens, in the order a teacher works
- The app follows the teaching cycle: **Plan → Teach → Assess → Progress** (योजना बनाएँ · पढ़ाएँ · आकलन · कक्षा प्रगति).
- Today's plan has three sections: **Before class** (groups, things to prepare, how to assess), **While teaching** (one tab per session slot) and **After teaching**.
- Long details are folded away. The suggested skill comes first on the Plan screen.
- A हिं / EN switch is on every screen, with natural English labels.

## One look per group
- Each of the three groups has one colour and one shape everywhere: ● Beginner, ▲ Progressor, ■ Proficient.
- Errors and notices use different colours, so they're never confused with a group.

## Recording levels while teaching
- During "You Do", the teacher records A / PA / NA for that group, listens to reading, or photographs worksheets, all without leaving the plan.
- Afterwards, Assess has two steps: **record today's performance → one decision for the next class**.
- Reading aloud: the app picks 4 children a day and rotates, so the whole group is heard within a few days.

## Features that now work everywhere
- Worksheets and reports open inside the app, which fixes the desktop failure.
- The CSV reports show as tables.
- Print offers a one-page summary or the full plan.

## Better worksheets and marking
- New worksheets for writing, dictation, composition and reading fluency.
- Hands-on skills use kit or everyday materials (pebbles, leaves, buttons), with a back-up the teacher can draw on the board, because most schools can't print.
- Photo marking:
  - refuses a photo taken under the wrong group;
  - names each mistake in plain words;
  - checks the writing task before giving a level.
- 9 new writing mistakes, reviewed by our education expert.

## Reports
- The class summary shows the skills taught and **where each child is (A / PA / NA)**.
- The mentor report shows results by skill.
- The CSVs are tidy, with readable columns.
