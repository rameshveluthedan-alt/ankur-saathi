## How much time do you have?
- **5 minutes to see the daily loop:** sign in as the demo teacher (**MPT101**, password 4826) and follow "Five sample inputs" below, starting with input 1. Everything you need is pre-recorded on this class: yesterday's work, the re-teach suggestion, a worked example. One person at a time: if two of you are on MPT101, the newer plan replaces the older one, and the group decisions and photos you see are the last ones that were pressed.
- **Two sessions to build the story yourself:** sign in as a **test teacher** (**MPT102 to MPT106**, 4826), use one each. Follow "A two-day evaluation" below. The nightly reset does not touch these classes, so what you record stays until you change it.

## Five sample inputs that show it at its best
1. **The daily loop, with a re-teach (about 3 minutes).** Sign in as MPT101 (password 4826). On **Plan**, choose the skill **N2.3.2 (adds two 2-digit numbers with regrouping)**. The group panel shows the three groups: the Beginners moved on to this skill, the Progressors are marked **re-teach** because 6 of 14 children made a mistake yesterday, and the Proficients continue with N3.3.1. Press **Generate**. *It takes 1 to 2.5 minutes: leave the screen open.* Then open **Yojana** (the plan): read the Progressors' re-teach block, the rotation with transition scripts and the "show me" questions. At the bottom open the Progressors' **worksheet** and use the browser's print preview: the roll-number answer grid, the carry tab above the tens column, and the one-line version to copy on the board where there is no printer.
2. **A curated skill bridge.** On Plan choose **N3.3.1 (adds 3-digit numbers with regrouping)**. The panel sends the Beginners back to **N2.3.2** and the Proficients on to **N3.3.6** (word problems), chosen by the education lead rather than by a formula. Change any group's skill with its drop-down.
3. **Hindi plans.** Choose **L2.3.1 (reads words with conjunct consonants)** and Generate: a reading plan in Hindi. Then choose **L2.7.2 (writes 3-5 connected sentences)**: a writing plan whose worksheet is reviewed from a photo (input 5).
4. **Recording a review with no AI, by observation or reading-aloud.** The Review screen offers only the way of recording that fits the skill. The three channels and the exact steps for each are in "Three ways to record" below.
5. **Photo review.** On **Review**, record "Advance" or "Re-teach" for a group first (the AI never goes first), then photograph or upload a worksheet. The steps are in "Three ways to record" below. [FILL ON FREEZE: link to 2 or 3 fictional sample worksheet photos to upload]

## Three ways to record (what the teacher taps on the Review screen)
The Review screen shows **three channels** — **✓ अवलोकन / Observed · 📸 फ़ोटो / Photo · ⏱️ पठन प्रवाह / Reading aloud** — and offers the ones that fit the plan's skill: a skill with no worksheet does not offer Photo, and reading-fluency skills do not offer Observed. A review decision for the group (Advance / Re-teach / Not sure) is recorded **before** any of the three channels: the AI never goes first.

- **✓ अवलोकन / Observed — no photo, no AI, no network call.** On Plan, choose **N0.1.1 (sorts objects by colour, shape or size)**, press Generate. On **Review**, record a decision for each group. Tap **✓ अवलोकन (Observed)**. For each child tap **✓ A (Achieved)**, **~ PA (Partial)** or **✗ NA (Not Achieved)**, and press **✓ अवलोकन जमा करें (Submit observations)**. Progress will show the children you tapped A for as achieved; children you tapped NA for will show "consider re-assessing" after a few days.
- **📸 फ़ोटो / Photo — the AI reads a worksheet and marks every sum.** Choose **N2.3.2** (input 1) and press Generate. On **Review**, record the Progressors' decision, tap **📸 फ़ोटो (Photo)**, then **इन बच्चों की फ़ोटो लें (Photograph these)** for the recommended sample (the app shows which rolls, so the paper trail is clear). Upload a worksheet (the fictional sample photos linked in input 5). Press **🔍 फ़ोटो का विश्लेषण करें (Analyse)**. The result shows each sum ticked or crossed, the mistake pattern it found, and children who still have "not yet" by roll number.
- **⏱️ पठन प्रवाह / Reading aloud — a reading-fluency count, no audio.** Choose **L2.5.1 (reads grade-level passages at 40 words a minute)** and press Generate. On **Review**, record the group's decision, tap **⏱️ पठन प्रवाह (Reading aloud)**, pick a roll number, type **CWPM (सही शब्द/मिनट — Correct Words Per Minute)** and the error count, and save. The app says where that child's reading sits. **No audio is recorded**: with several groups at work a recording would be too noisy to be clear, so the daily review is by observation and the teacher times the reading herself. Voice is planned for the periodic assessments (`docs/ROADMAP.md`).

## A two-day evaluation (optional, about 15 minutes across two sessions)
Ankur Saathi's point is that **the next day looks at yesterday**. On MPT101 this is pre-recorded. To see it built from your own actions, use a test teacher (MPT102 to MPT106). Use one each: if two of you share one, Day 1's state is whoever acted last.

- **Day 1 (about 10 minutes).** Sign in with your test teacher. On Plan, open **Numeracy**, pick an unused skill so you do not collide with another reviewer: **N2.2.1** for MPT102, **N2.2.2** for MPT103, **N2.2.4** for MPT104, **L2.5.1** for MPT105, **L2.3.2** for MPT106. Generate the plan. Open the Progressors' worksheet, look through it, close it. On **Review**, record a decision for the Progressors and record evidence for three or four children on the chosen skill (any channel; **✓ अवलोकन (Observed)** is fastest).
- **Day 2 (about 5 minutes), the next working day between 02:00 India time and midnight.** Sign in with the same test teacher. On Plan, the **"yesterday" line and the re-teach suggestion are written from your own evidence of Day 1** (for instance "yesterday, 2 of 4 made a mistake, suggests re-teach"). Choose the suggestion, generate today's plan, and you have seen the full arc that makes this product different.

## What to expect
- **A plan is written by an AI model, so two runs differ.** Code checks every sum, answer key and number range afterwards, and drops "show me" questions that are wrong or guessable.
- **Limits protect the credits.** If a daily limit is reached, these skills still show a recorded sample plan (labelled "sample plan"): **N0.1.1, N2.2.1, N2.3.2, L2.3.1, L2.5.1, L2.7.2**; other skills say the limit was reached and that it resets the next day. Observation and reading aloud (input 4) need no AI at all.
- **MPT101 is a single, shared class.** If more than one of you is on MPT101 at the same time, the plan and the group decisions you see are the last ones that were pressed; your own may be replaced by someone else's. Nothing lasting is lost, and MPT102 to MPT106 are separate classes, one per reviewer, that stay yours between sessions.
- **The demo resets every night at 01:30 India time** (MPT101's history is restored to the recorded N2.3.2 story; the test teachers MPT102 to MPT106 are not touched; accounts keep their passwords).
- **Slow or no network:** the whole first load is under 90 KB. To try it: generate a plan (step 1 above), switch on airplane mode (or DevTools → Network → Offline) and reload. The app opens, a notice says you are offline, today's plan and its worksheets still open and print, and Generate and photo sending are switched off with the reason. Recording decisions, observations and reading scores need the internet (nothing is queued yet). Details: [docs/OFFLINE.md](OFFLINE.md). [FILL ON FREEZE: repeat this on the frozen version and confirm]
