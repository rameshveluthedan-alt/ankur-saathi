## How much time do you have?
- **5 minutes to see the daily loop:** sign in as the demo teacher (**MPT101**, password 4826) and follow "Five sample inputs" below, starting with input 1. Everything you need is pre-recorded on this class: yesterday's work, the re-teach suggestion, a worked example. One person at a time: if two of you are on MPT101, the newer plan replaces the older one, and the group decisions and photos you see are the last ones that were pressed.
- **Two sessions to build the story yourself:** sign in as a **test teacher** (**MPT102 to MPT106**, 4826), use one each. Follow "A two-day evaluation" below. The nightly reset does not touch these classes, so what you record stays until you change it.

## Five sample inputs that show it at its best
1. **The daily loop, with a re-teach (about 3 minutes).** Sign in as MPT101 (password 4826). On **योजना बनाएँ (Make plan)**, choose the skill **N2.3.2 (adds two 2-digit numbers with regrouping)**. The group panel shows the three groups: the Beginners moved on to this skill, the Progressors are marked **re-teach** because 6 of 14 children made a mistake yesterday, and the Proficients continue with N3.3.1. Press **📋 आज की योजना बनाएँ (Make today's plan)**. *It takes 1 to 2.5 minutes: leave the screen open.* Then open **आज की योजना (Today's plan)**: read the Progressors' re-teach block, the rotation with transition scripts and the "show me" questions. At the bottom open the Progressors' **worksheet** and use the browser's print preview: the roll-number answer grid, the carry tab above the tens column, and the one-line version to copy on the board where there is no printer.
2. **A curated skill bridge.** On **योजना बनाएँ** choose **N3.3.1 (adds 3-digit numbers with regrouping)**. The panel sends the Beginners back to **N2.3.2** and the Proficients on to **N3.3.6** (word problems), chosen by the education lead rather than by a formula. Change any group's skill with its drop-down.
3. **Hindi plans.** Choose **L2.3.1 (reads words with conjunct consonants)** and make today's plan: a reading plan in Hindi. Then choose **L2.7.2 (writes 3-5 connected sentences)**: a writing plan whose worksheet is reviewed from a photo (input 5).
4. **Recording a review with no AI, by observation or reading-aloud.** The Review screen offers only the way of recording that fits the skill. The three channels and the exact steps for each are in "Three ways to record" below.
5. **Photo review.** On **समीक्षा (Review)**, record your first judgment for each group first (⬆ Advance, 🔄 Re-teach or ❓ Not sure: the AI never goes first), then photograph or upload a worksheet. There are no ready-made photos to download, on purpose: the AI marks a photo against the answer key of **your own** plan's worksheet, whose numbers differ from plan to plan. The steps to make a photo in two minutes are in "Test the photo review with a sheet of your own" below.

## Three ways to record: the Review's three steps (what the teacher taps on समीक्षा)
The Review has **three steps**. **1 · पहला अनुमान (first judgment):** for every group, ⬆ आगे बढ़ें (Advance), 🔄 दोहराएँ (Re-teach) or ❓ पक्का नहीं (Not sure), recorded **before** the AI reads anything. **2 · बच्चों का काम देखें (the children's work):** checked in the way that fits the skill (below). **3 · अंतिम निर्णय (final decision):** the teacher decides again with her first judgment and today's evidence in front of her; tomorrow's plan follows from it. Step 2 offers **✓ देखकर दर्ज करें (Observe and record) · 📸 वर्कशीट की फ़ोटो (Worksheet photo) · ⏱️ पढ़कर सुनाना (Reading aloud)**, only the ones that fit the plan's skill; "ⓘ यही तरीका क्यों?" says why. The letters **A / PA / NA** are explained on every screen that shows them: A = did it without help · PA = with help, or partly correct · NA = not yet.

- **✓ देखकर दर्ज करें / Observe and record: no photo, no AI, no network call.** On **योजना बनाएँ**, choose **N0.1.1 (sorts objects by colour, shape or size)** and make today's plan. On **समीक्षा**, record a first judgment for each group (step 1), then open **✓ देखकर दर्ज करें**. For each child tap **A**, **PA** or **NA**, and press **✓ जमा करें (Submit)**. A child already recorded today shows what was recorded, and a change is shown as "PA → A". Class progress shows the children you tapped A for as achieved; children you tapped NA for show "consider re-assessing" after a few days. *In the lesson itself:* on **आज की योजना**, open a group's teach block and tap **अभी दर्ज करें** under its "तुम करोगे" (You Do): the same list opens at once, and **टिक शीट** (a paper sheet to tick during the lesson) can be printed from the "आज की समीक्षा" fold.
- **📸 वर्कशीट की फ़ोटो / Worksheet photo: the AI reads a worksheet and marks every sum.** Choose **N2.3.2** (input 1) and make today's plan. On **समीक्षा**, record the first judgments (step 1), open **📸 वर्कशीट की फ़ोटो**, and take or choose photos of the suggested sample (the app shows which roll numbers). Upload a worksheet (the fictional sample photos linked in input 5) and press **🔍 फ़ोटो का विश्लेषण करें (Analyse)**. The result shows each sum ticked or crossed, the mistake pattern it found, and children who still have "not yet" by roll number. Then go on to step 3.
- **⏱️ पढ़कर सुनाना / Reading aloud: a reading-fluency count, no audio.** Choose **L2.5.1 (reads grade-level passages at 40 words a minute)** and make today's plan. On **समीक्षा**, record the first judgments, open **⏱️ पढ़कर सुनाना**, read the text shown (one the class has not read today), pick a roll number, type the **सही शब्द/मिनट (correct words per minute)** and the number of mistakes, and press **⏱️ पठन दर्ज करें**. The app says where that child's reading sits. **No audio is recorded**: with several groups at work a recording would be too noisy to be clear, so the daily review is by observation and the teacher times the reading herself. Voice is planned for the periodic assessments (`docs/ROADMAP.md`).

## Test the photo review with a sheet of your own (about 5 minutes, no printer needed)
The photo review marks a worksheet photo against the answer key of the plan you generated, so a photo made elsewhere would be marked against the wrong sums. Make the photo from your own plan:
1. **Sign in as a test teacher (MPT102 to MPT106)**, not MPT101: on the shared MPT101 another reviewer's new plan would replace yours. On **योजना बनाएँ** choose **N2.3.2** and make today's plan.
2. On **आज की योजना**, open the **Progressors'** worksheet (at the bottom). **Print it** and fill it in by hand, or, with no printer, copy the numbered problems into a notebook page (the board line under each group's "तुम करोगे" gives them). Write **a roll number from the sample the Review suggests** at the top (the roll number is the only thing to write: no name).
3. **Fill it in like a child**, with some mistakes on purpose. Easy ones to try (for 34 + 28): the right answer **62**; the carry forgotten, **52**; the whole ones sum written, **512**; the wrong digit carried, **72**; one number copied differently from the sheet (say 24 + 28 for 34 + 28); and an extra left blank. A crooked or dim photo is itself a fair test.
4. On **समीक्षा**: record the first judgment for each group (step 1), open **📸 वर्कशीट की फ़ोटो**, choose **the Progressors**, take or choose the photo, and press **🔍 फ़ोटो का विश्लेषण करें**.
5. **What to expect.** The roll number and each answer are read, and each sum is ticked or crossed against the key. We tested this on five sheets (`docs/vision/`): the numbers were read almost perfectly, but **the mistake pattern is named roughly** (it can say "dropped the carry" and "wrote the whole ones sum" together, and may miss "wrong digit carried"), and a copied-differently problem may be missed. The teacher decides: nothing changes a child's level.
6. If the day's **AI limit** has been reached, the app says so in plain words (a photo cannot be faked with a recorded plan); try again the next day.

## Reports and printouts (on Class progress, and on today's plan)
- **📋 कक्षा सारांश / Class summary** (Class progress → **📄 रिपोर्ट**): the teacher's own page for planning next week. The last 4 weeks, per subject: how the groups stand, what was taught and the final decisions, each group's suggested next skill, **the children to look at** (stuck on a skill after 3 or more days, not checked for 2 weeks, "not yet" on 2 or more days) with their names (shown on the teacher's own phone only), and the most common mistakes with a matching activity. Printable. Each opening is logged, because the page shows names.
- **💬 मेंटर रिपोर्ट / Mentor report** (Class progress → **📄 रिपोर्ट**): a one-page report for a visiting block or cluster officer, **roll numbers only, never a name**. It leads with talking points (one strength, then up to two things to work on, then a question for the teacher), then how the teacher works (plans made, reviews completed, decisions with evidence), the children's progress and the common mistakes. The points come from fixed rules that are printed on the page, **not from AI**. On a class with only a few days of use it shows little; it fills out as days are recorded.
- **Printed plan and tick sheet** (on **आज की योजना**): **🖨️ प्रिंट** gives one A4 page to carry; **टिक शीट** gives a paper table (roll number, A / PA / NA, or the words read correctly) to tick during the lesson.
- **CSV files** (Class progress → **📄 रिपोर्ट**, "डेटा, CSV फ़ाइल"): the class report with names, and the officer data with roll numbers only.

## A two-day evaluation (optional, about 15 minutes across two sessions)
Ankur Saathi's point is that **the next day looks at yesterday**. On MPT101 this is pre-recorded. To see it built from your own actions, use a test teacher (MPT102 to MPT106). Use one each: if two of you share one, Day 1's state is whoever acted last.

- **Day 1 (about 10 minutes).** Sign in with your test teacher. On **योजना बनाएँ**, open **Numeracy**, pick an unused skill so you do not collide with another reviewer: **N2.2.1** for MPT102, **N2.2.2** for MPT103, **N2.2.4** for MPT104, **L2.5.1** for MPT105, **L2.3.2** for MPT106. Make today's plan. Open the Progressors' worksheet, look through it, close it. On **Review**, record a first judgment for each group (step 1), then evidence for three or four children on the chosen skill (step 2, any way; **✓ देखकर दर्ज करें (Observe and record)** is fastest), then a final decision (step 3).
- **Day 2 (about 5 minutes), the next working day between 02:00 India time and midnight.** Sign in with the same test teacher. On **योजना बनाएँ**, the **"yesterday" line and the re-teach suggestion are written from your own evidence of Day 1** (for instance "yesterday, 2 of 4 made a mistake, suggests re-teach"). Choose the suggestion, generate today's plan, and you have seen the full arc that makes this product different.

## What to expect
- **A plan is written by an AI model, so two runs differ.** Code checks every sum, answer key and number range afterwards, and drops "show me" questions that are wrong or guessable.
- **The Review's step 3 (final decision) can be recorded without any evidence, by design.** The teacher decides and the evidence only informs her: a group nobody has checked shows "अभी किसी का आकलन नहीं" (nobody assessed yet), the app makes no re-teach suggestion for it, and her decision still shapes tomorrow's plan. It is not required to photograph, tap or time a reading first, because some children are absent, a phone may have no signal (photos need the internet), and some skills have no photo at all.
- **Each skill on योजना बनाएँ shows two things.** How it is checked, with the same icons as the Review: **📸 फ़ोटो** (a worksheet photo), **✓ देखकर** (observation), **⏱️ पढ़कर** (reading aloud). And a label for how much testing stands behind it: ✔ परखा हुआ (tested), ◐ AI-लिखित सामग्री (AI-written content), ⚠ प्रयोगात्मक (experimental), ○ अभी परखा नहीं (not yet tested). **Most skills say "not yet tested"**: the real-model test runs covered 27 of the 101 skills, and the labels have not been refreshed from them (`docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md`). Every skill still gets a plan.
- **Limits protect the credits.** If a daily limit is reached, these skills still show a recorded sample plan (labelled "sample plan"): **N0.1.1, N2.2.1, N2.3.2, L2.3.1, L2.5.1, L2.7.2**; other skills say the limit was reached and that it resets the next day. Observation and reading aloud (input 4) need no AI at all.
- **MPT101 is a single, shared class.** If more than one of you is on MPT101 at the same time, the plan and the group decisions you see are the last ones that were pressed; your own may be replaced by someone else's. Nothing lasting is lost, and MPT102 to MPT106 are separate classes, one per reviewer, that stay yours between sessions.
- **The demo resets every night at 01:30 India time** (MPT101's history is restored to the recorded N2.3.2 story; the test teachers MPT102 to MPT106 are not touched; accounts keep their passwords).
- **Slow or no network:** the whole first load is about 120 KB (compressed). To try it: generate a plan (step 1 above), switch on airplane mode (or DevTools → Network → Offline) and reload. The app opens, a notice says you are offline, today's plan and its worksheets still open and print, and Generate and photo sending are switched off with the reason. Recording decisions, observations and reading scores need the internet (nothing is queued yet). Details: [docs/OFFLINE.md](OFFLINE.md). [FILL ON FREEZE: repeat this on the frozen version and confirm]
- **What is not built** is listed openly in [docs/STATUS.md](STATUS.md); the plan for roles (state, district, school) is in [docs/ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).
